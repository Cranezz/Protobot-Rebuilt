# Protobot Rebuilt V2, Beta 1.9 (build 45)

- **Controller screen** (Edit > Controller…, or Devices > Controller): a picture of the V5 Controller with every stick
  direction and button labelled, Steam style. Click one and press a key or a gamepad button to bind it. Presets for
  tank, arcade and split arcade, what each button runs on your robot, and "Put on it" to give a button a motor or
  cylinder. Bindings are saved with each robot ("Use as my default" for robots without their own). Gamepad sticks are
  analog: half a push drives at about half speed.
- **Skills replays:** every run is recorded. Watch it from the results (Watch replay), open saved ones with File > Open
  replay…, and watch other players' best runs from the skills leaderboard.
- **Leaderboard:** scores can only be posted by a checked run now. Report a run that breaks the rules (its replay goes
  with your report), or appeal a decision from the Account page.
- **Demo robot:** Sample Builds/Override Demo Robot.pbb, a legal Override robot on the 6-motor drive: drive into a lying
  pin or cup with the intake on (I) to pick it up, K to drop it; the arm (U/J) holds where you leave it.
- **Motors can hold:** a motor's card in Devices has Brake/Hold: Hold keeps an arm or lift where it stopped.
- **Play game:** the default start now finds a legal spot clear of the pins and cups by the wall (bigger robots started
  on top of them).
- **Parts list:** families open in place again, and their kinds open as smaller rows inside them.
- **Saw:** motors and electronics can't be cut (VEX's rules don't allow it).
- Fixed: the build tools showing during game setup after a run.
