# Protobot Rebuilt V2, v2.0 Beta 1 (build 74)

**Choose your ports.**

- **Port picker:** in the Devices window (Edit > Devices…), click a device's port number to put it on another port. A device already on that port swaps with it. Ports are kept with your build, and Insert devices writes your code to match. (This replaces choosing ports with Smart Cables, which were taken out in build 73.)
- Fixed: a build opened in the same moment as another could give its motors the wrong ports.
