# Protobot Rebuilt V2, Beta 1.9 (build 38)

- **Accounts:** Multiplayer > Account. Play as a guest, or make an account (username and password) to keep your
  stats and scores and sign in on any computer. Making an account keeps what you did as a guest.
- **Stats:** matches, wins, losses, ties, points and your best skills score, kept on the server.
- **Skills leaderboard:** finish a legal Robot Skills run and it's posted, after the server checks it (robot size,
  run length, the scoring adds up).
- **Voice chat in online matches:** you hear only your alliance partner. Push to talk (hold T), open mic or off,
  mute a player for yourself; no microphone needed to play.
- **Devices panel:** the robot's motors, sensors and pneumatics laid out like the V5 Brain's screens, with working
  sensors; resizable, in columns.
