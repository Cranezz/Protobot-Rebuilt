# Protobot Rebuilt V2, v2.0 Beta 1 (build 85)

**Updates show up while Protobot is open, the developer can ask you questions, and Protobot shows on your Discord profile.**

- **Live updates:** when a new version comes out, an "Update available" card appears in the top right within about a minute, even if Protobot has been open for hours. Save and update now (it saves your build first), What's new or Later. It waits until a game run or an online match is over.
- **Messages from the developer:** sometimes a card asks for your opinion on a feature, with a box to type your answer. Press Send and it goes straight to the developer (with the name you play under), or Not now to skip it. Each one shows once.
- **Discord status:** with the Discord app running, your profile shows "Playing Protobot Rebuilt V2" and what you're doing (building, simulating, a game, writing code). Settings > Preferences > Discord turns it off, or adds your build's name.
- **Easier downloads:** the release page starts with a Download table: Setup.exe for Windows, the zip for Mac. The other files are for automatic updates.
