# Protobot Rebuilt V2, Beta 1.9 (build 40)

- **Updating works:** updates now install and restart Protobot by themselves (before, an update could stop at 100%
  and Protobot reopened on the old version). If you're on build 37 or 38 and the update gets stuck, download
  **ProtobotRebuiltV2-win-Setup.exe** below once; from then on updates install on their own.
- **A proper update screen:** "Update available" shows what's new and the download size; updating shows a progress
  bar with the megabytes, then installs and restarts.
- **Intro:** Protobot opens with a short animation over the field that blends into the home menu (any key or click
  skips it).
