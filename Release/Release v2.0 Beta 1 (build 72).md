# Protobot Rebuilt V2, v2.0 Beta 1 (build 72)

**Version 2.0 Beta 1: privacy, safety and a home on Discord.**

- **New name for versions:** this is v2.0 Beta 1 (build 72). Updates count up from here.
- **Beta tester badge:** play Protobot for 10 minutes during the beta to earn a permanent "Beta tester" badge. Make an account to claim it (a guest is asked to): it stays with your account and shows beside your name in the server list, lobbies and the in-match Players window.
- **Your profile on the home menu:** your name, badges and whether you're signed in sit at the bottom left of the home menu. Click it to open your Profile page: sign in or make an account, see your stats and badges.
- **Official Discord server:** guides, help, bug reports and builds from other players, with announcements of big updates.
- **Privacy Policy and Terms of Use:** in Help, on the Profile page and in the new question before you go online the first time. They say what Protobot keeps and sends, who sees it and how to delete it.
- **Before you play online:** Protobot asks the month and year you were born (not the day), once. Making an account asks for your birthday too (required by children's privacy law) and keeps it with the account, so it follows you to another computer. Neither can be changed later.
- **Players under 13:** can play online and make an account, but voice chat is off (they don't hear it and aren't heard), and a crossed-out speaker by their name tells teammates. They play under a made-up name like "Speedy Gear 42" ("New" picks another), their username is only for signing in, their robot shows as "Robot", and their runs aren't shared as replays. When they turn 13, voice chat and their own name switch on by themselves.
- **Delete my online data** (Multiplayer > Profile): deletes your guest player or account and everything kept online for it (stats, scores, shared replays).
- **Name filter:** player names, usernames, match names and robot names with offensive words are refused, and ones from older versions are hidden. Usernames can't be email addresses.
- **Can't hear voice chat:** anyone with voice chat off shows the crossed-out speaker in the lobby, the Players window and the voice panel.
- **Reports are safer:** bug reports, player reports and appeals go through Protobot's server to the moderators, not straight from the app, with a limit on how many can be sent. Their logs no longer contain your computer's user name, and the report window says exactly what's included.
- **Your files moved:** Protobot's data folder (custom parts, autosaves, replays, logs) and settings are now under "Protobot" instead of the developer's name. They're moved for you the first time v2.0 starts; nothing is lost.
- **Credits and licenses:** Help > Credits and licenses. Protobot is a fan-made tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by VEX Robotics or the REC Foundation.
- Hole-face outlines are drawn by Protobot's own outline code (a paid third-party outline package was removed).
