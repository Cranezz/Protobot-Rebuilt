# Protobot Rebuilt V2, v2.0 Beta 1 (build 78)

**Copy and paste, bending plates, drilling into shafts, and tutorial videos.**

- Copy and paste: select a part and press Ctrl+C, then Ctrl+V to place a copy. It copies everything screwed to the part too, like a wheel with the gear bolted to it, and the copy snaps onto screws, shafts and holes like a part from the parts list. Click to place more; Esc stops.
- The Bend tool (7) now bends plates as well as high strength shafts and standoffs. The holes go round the bend with the metal.
- The Drill (5) on a high strength shaft or standoff: click its side for a hole straight across (it snaps to the middle), or its end to drill into it. With a 9/64" bit or smaller the hole is tapped for an 8-32 screw.
- Mirror (M) now copies the shafts that run through what you mirror, with the gears and spacers on them, and the mirror line snaps to the middle of your build, so a drivetrain side mirrors exactly onto the other side.
- Fixed: placing a wheel on a screw right beside a gear could leave the wheel turned the wrong way.
- Fixed: holes drilled across a long shaft came out as long slots.
- Fixed: a drilled hole kept showing its old square outline while the part was selected.
- Fixed: in the plastic sketcher, choosing a plastic could also pick or unpick an area of the piece behind the list.
- Tutorial videos: 12 short videos, from getting started to robot code and online play, are in the Discord's #tutorials channel and at https://github.com/Cranezz/Protobot-Rebuilt/releases/tag/tutorials
