# Protobot Rebuilt V2, v2.0 Beta 1 (build 77)

**Settings in one place, and a clearer view when sketching on a part.**

- One Settings window: Preferences (general, camera speeds, parts list), Graphics, Keyboard shortcuts and Controller are tabs across its top. Open it with the gear at the top right of the screen or Edit > Settings…. The camera speeds are under Preferences > Camera.
- Sketching a plastic piece on a part: "Show only this part" (top bar of the sketcher) hides the rest of the robot so only the part you're sketching on shows and can be picked. Click "Show all parts" to bring everything back; your choice is remembered.
