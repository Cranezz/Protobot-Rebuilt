# Privacy Policy

Protobot Rebuilt V2 ("Protobot") is a free, open-source robot building program made by a volunteer developer (GitHub user Cranezz). This policy says what Protobot keeps, what it sends, who sees it and how to have it deleted. Effective 22 November 2026 (updated: usage time, see below).

# The short version

- Building, simulating, robot code and offline games happen on your computer. Your builds and code aren't sent anywhere. Once you've agreed to these terms, Protobot sends how long it's used and on which parts of the app (see "Usage time").
- Going online (Multiplayer, the skills leaderboard) uses Unity Gaming Services. Unity keeps a player id, the name you play under, your stats and scores, and, if you make an account, its username and your birth month and year.
- Passwords go straight to Unity's sign-in service. Unity stores them hashed (scrambled one way): nobody can read them, not the developer and not other players.
- Players under 13 can play online and make an account, but without voice chat and under a made-up name.
- You can delete everything kept online about you from inside Protobot: the Profile page (click your name at the bottom left of the home menu, or Multiplayer > Profile): Delete my online data.
- There are no ads. Your data is never sold or shared for advertising.

# What stays on your computer

These are kept in your user folder (AppData) and never leave it unless you send a bug report:
- your builds, autosaves, custom parts, saved groups, replays and robot code
- Protobot's settings, including the month and year you were born (from the question before you first go online; not the day)
- a session log of what you did in Protobot (each tool, click and key, but not what you type in text boxes) and the errors, used for bug reports

# What goes online, and who sees it

**Unity Gaming Services** (run by Unity Technologies; their privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy):
- **Player id:** a random id for this computer's guest player, or your account. Unity also sees your internet (IP) address when Protobot connects, as every online service does.
- **Account (optional, any age):** a username, a password and your birth month and year. The username can't be an email address. Players 13 and older play under their username; a younger player's username is only for signing in and nobody else sees it. The birth month and year are kept with the account so the rules for players under 13 follow you to another computer and end by themselves when you turn 13. They can't be changed afterward.
- **Your name in matches:** the name you choose (players under 13 get a made-up one, like "Speedy Gear 42"). Others in the server list, the lobby and the match see it, and your robot's name (just "Robot" for players under 13).
- **Matches:** your robot (its build and custom parts) and its moves go to the other players in that match while it's played, through Unity's relay. They're not kept afterward, except in a report (below).
- **Stats and leaderboard:** matches won, lost and tied, points and skills scores. The leaderboard shows your name, score and robot name to everyone.
- **Usage time (after you agree to these terms):** while Protobot is open and being used, once a minute it sends how many seconds were spent on each part of the app (the home menu, building, simulating, games, multiplayer, robot code, sketching, replays), with your player id, the name you play under and whether it's an account. Unity keeps your total, the time per part and per day (the last 60 days), and when you were first and last online. Only the developer sees it, to learn how Protobot is used and what to improve, and to show the number of players online on the home menu (just a number). It's never shown to other players, sold or used for advertising, and Delete my online data deletes it.
- **Badges:** badges your account has earned (like Beta tester, for playing 10 minutes during the beta: Protobot counts the time on your computer and the badge is claimed with an account). Other players see them beside your name in the server list and lobbies.
- **Shared replays:** your best skills run on the leaderboard, so others can watch it. It holds your robot and its program. Players under 13 don't share replays.
- **Voice chat (13 and older):** in online matches you can talk to your alliance partner through Unity's Vivox service. Voice is sent live and isn't recorded or kept by Protobot. You can switch it off. Players under 13 don't hear voice chat and aren't heard; a crossed-out speaker by their name tells their teammates.

**Reports** (bug reports, reports of a player, appeals), only when you press Send:
- They go to the developer's private Discord channel, through Unity's servers. Only the developer and any moderators they add can read them.
- A bug report holds what you wrote, the session logs (with your computer's user name taken out), your build if you leave that switched on, a picture of the screen if you leave that on, Protobot's version, and your computer's OS, processor, memory and graphics card.
- A player report holds what you wrote, both players' names and ids, the match record (where each robot was) and the reported robot's custom parts.
- Please don't put your name, email address or other personal details in a report.
- Reports are kept only as long as they're needed to fix the bug or deal with the report. Ask for one to be deleted (see "Contact").

**Updates:** Protobot checks GitHub (github.com/Cranezz/Protobot-Rebuilt) for a new version. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any download.

# Players under 13

Protobot is used by young robotics students, so it follows the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Before going online the first time, Protobot asks for the month and year you were born, and making an account asks again. For players under 13:
- voice chat is off: they don't hear others and aren't heard
- they play under a made-up name instead of one they type, and their robot shows as "Robot"
- an account's username is only for signing in and isn't shown to anyone
- their runs aren't kept as shared replays
They can still play matches, keep stats and appear on the skills leaderboard under the made-up name. When they turn 13, voice chat and their own name switch on by themselves. If you're a parent and think your child gave personal information anyway, contact the developer (below) and it will be deleted.

# Deleting your data

- **In Protobot:** the Profile page (click your name at the bottom left of the home menu, or Multiplayer > Profile): Delete my online data deletes your player or account, your stats, badges, usage time, birth month and year, shared replays and leaderboard scores (the scores disappear at once and are removed for good by the developer within 30 days).
- **On your computer:** uninstall Protobot and delete its data folder (Help > Open bug log folder opens its Logs folder; delete the folder above it).
- **Anything else** (a report you sent): ask the developer (below).

# Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU and UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA), you can ask to see, correct or delete the data kept about you, and complain to your data protection authority. The data is used only to run the online features you choose to use, to apply the rules for players under 13, and to fix bugs and moderate. Unity Gaming Services and Discord may process it outside your country.

# Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, Protobot will ask you to read and agree to it again before going online.

# Contact

Open an issue at https://github.com/Cranezz/Protobot-Rebuilt/issues or use Help > Report a bug in Protobot. For a private request (such as deleting a report), say so in the report and it will be handled privately.
