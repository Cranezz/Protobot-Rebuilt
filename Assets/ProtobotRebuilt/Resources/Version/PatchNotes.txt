# Protobot Rebuilt V2, Beta 1.9 (build 69)

- **Odometry robot:** a new sample build with tracking-wheel pods (a Rotation sensor as the arm, a rubber band holding
  the wheel down) and an Inertial sensor. Unpowered tracking wheels now roll true (within 0.1-0.3% of the real
  distance), and tracking wheels no longer count up while the robot stands still (before a run or after a stop). A Rotation
  sensor read the moment a program starts gives its real angle (it gave the last run's).
- **New example, "Odometry: 4 points and back":** drives to four points, each with its own heading, stops 2 s at
  each and writes down how close it got (in the output and on the Brain's screen), then comes home. On the odometry
  sample robot the four points come out within about 1/4" and a fifth of a degree (home, after a minute and a
  half of driving, within about 2/3": odometry drifts a little over time, as on a real robot). It lines up with each goal's heading before driving
  in, and tries again if it misses, aiming to make up for the miss.
- **Tracking wheels stay upright:** they leaned about 1.5 degrees in their Rotation sensors while driving; now they
  sit square, as built.
- **Insert devices measures from the middle of the drive wheels:** tracking wheel and GPS offsets were measured
  from the drive motors, which can sit inches from the wheels on a geared drive.
- **Quieter motors:** V5 motors are quiet, and now they sound it. Several running motors no longer add up to a roar.
- **Python fix:** a line that continued inside brackets (a long print(...) over two lines) could break the line
  after it ("expected the end of the line").
- **Robots stop where you let go:** brake mode really brakes, so a robot doesn't keep turning after the sticks go.
- **Every sensor wired:** 3-Wire Expanders, Accelerometers and every 3-wire sensor work in Python, VEXcode C++ and
  PROS. Devices keep their ports when you reopen a build.
- **Games, the robot:** a robot needs a V5 Brain, and only what's connected to it goes on the field (stray parts
  elsewhere in the build stay behind). A Pin saved on your robot is your Preload, where you put it; more game
  elements than that must go first.
- **Games, setup:** place the Preload anywhere on the robot ("Place on the robot…": click a face, R stands it up or
  lays it down, Q/E turn it). The setup shows your robot's height. Saved starts show their names. "Clear of the
  Toggle" checks real contact. Start run stays at the bottom of the panel. No grid or axis lines on the field.
- **Loaders:** raise and lower the Loader (a robot takes Match Loads out of its bottom), and a Loader only takes what
  fits in it. Matches have 10 alliance Pins per Alliance, and Match Loads go in during driver control only.
- **Toggles** lift in their brackets and only count when they're fully seated. They keep their colors in replays.
- **Replays:** "Watch a replay" on the home menu, and double-clicking a .pbr (or .pbb) file someone sent opens it in
  Protobot.
- **Cables:** the GPS, Inertial, Vision Sensor, 3-Wire Expander and 5.5W motor have their real smart ports (cables
  went anywhere on them before).
- **Pneumatics:** a cylinder's rod moves with it when you move the cylinder, and tubing can be routed over cylinder
  mounts and rod pivots (their screw holes were taken for air ports).
- **Motors sound like V5 motors:** the gear whine follows the motor's speed, and a motor straining against a load
  whines like the real one.
- **Faster with many Vision Sensors:** cards share their picture updates, so nine open sensors don't slow the app.
- **Multiplayer:** signing in shows your account name, and your match is "<name>'s match". Report a player for
  unneeded or oversized custom parts (the report carries the parts). The background no longer shifts when you
  switch screens.
- **Home menu:** Report a bug opens over it, Patch Notes shows these notes, and the Credits have a V2 section.

# Protobot Rebuilt V2, Beta 1.9 (build 65)

- **C++ robot code:** write PROS (with LemLib or EZ-Template) or VEXcode V5 C++ and run it on the simulated robot, the
  same code you'd download to the real one. Python and VEXcode Blocks work too.
- **EZ-Template:** PID drive, turn and swing, exit conditions, motion chaining, odometry with pure pursuit and
  boomerang moves, and the autonomous selector on the Brain screen. v3 and v2 names both work.
- **LemLib:** 0.4, 0.5 and today's versions, including path files from path.jerryio.
- **Whole projects:** Code > Project > Open a project folder loads a PROS or VEXcode project with all its files.
  Errors name the file and line.
- **VEXcode Blocks:** drag-and-drop blocks laid out like VEXcode's: My Blocks, lists, comments. "Show the Python" or
  "Convert to a text program" when you're ready.
- **Debugging:** the Monitor tab shows each thread, the line it's on and your variables. Pause (F6) and Step (F10)
  the robot. Console colors work.
- **VS Code:** opens your code with autocomplete for PROS, VEXcode C++, LemLib and EZ-Template.
- **Sensors simulated:** Vision Sensor (signatures, color codes, the Vision Utility), GPS Sensor (reads the field
  strip, with its quality), and the new AI Vision Sensor (colors, codes, and the game's pins and cups found by its AI
  model). The AI Vision Sensor is now in the parts list with VEX's own model.
- **Insert devices** writes the configuration for every sensor, including where the GPS is mounted.
- **Gamepads and USB joysticks** drive the robot; the controller screen shows each button and what it does.
- **Online matches:** each player's robot runs its own program in autonomous, then driver control.
- **Driving:** omni wheels slide sideways like real ones, and a robot standing still no longer creeps.

# Protobot Rebuilt V2, Beta 1.9 (build 50)

- **Robot code** (Code in the top bar): write your robot's program in Python, VEXcode V5 Python style, and run it on the
  simulated robot. Line numbers, colors, auto-indent, Ctrl+/ to comment, F5 to run, errors shown on their line.
  "VS Code" opens it in your own VS Code (with autocomplete); saving there updates the app. The program is saved with
  the build.
- **Insert devices:** one click writes your robot's configuration at the top of the program: every motor on its port
  with its cartridge and direction, the drive's motor groups and a drivetrain measured from your build (wheel size,
  track width, gear ratio), sensors and pneumatics.
- **Brain screen:** the program can print and draw on the V5 Brain's screen (rectangles, circles, lines, colors, fonts)
  and you can tap it (autonomous selectors work). The controller's screen shows beside it, and rumble shakes it (and
  your gamepad).
- **Tuning graph:** the Graph tab plots the motors your program drives (speed or position against what it asked for)
  and anything it prints as `name: number`, for tuning PID. The same code runs on the real robot.
- **Motion library** (`from motion import *`): odometry and autonomous moves with LemLib's names: move_to_point,
  turn_to_heading, move_to_pose (boomerang), swing turns, pure pursuit along a path, motion chaining, tracking wheels or
  drive motors plus the Inertial sensor, and LemLib's PID settings and exit conditions.
- **Examples** (12): competition template, tank/arcade drive, PID tuning, odometry and motions, an autonomous selector
  on the Brain's screen, controller screen and rumble, and more. They fill in your own robot's devices.
- **Play game:** match autonomous runs your program, then its driver control. New mode **Programming Skills**: a skills
  run driven only by your program, with its own leaderboard (the program goes with the shared replay).
- **Smoother:** the game screens run much faster (on an RTX 3070 Ti at 1440p, 250+ FPS while driving on the full
  field, no frame drops). Replays no longer drop to 30 FPS.
- **Driving feels truer:** omni wheels grip under full power (they used to spin), and robots don't spin slowly after
  coasting to a stop.

# Protobot Rebuilt V2, Beta 1.9 (build 45)

- **Controller screen** (Edit > Controller…, or Devices > Controller): a picture of the V5 Controller with every stick
  direction and button labelled, Steam style. Click one and press a key or a gamepad button to bind it. Presets for
  tank, arcade and split arcade, what each button runs on your robot, and "Put on it" to give a button a motor or
  cylinder. Bindings are saved with each robot ("Use as my default" for robots without their own). Gamepad sticks are
  analog: half a push drives at about half speed.
- **Skills replays:** every run is recorded. Watch it from the results (Watch replay), open saved ones with File > Open
  replay…, and watch other players' best runs from the skills leaderboard.
- **Leaderboard:** scores can only be posted by a checked run now. Report a run that breaks the rules (its replay goes
  with your report), or appeal a decision from the Account page.
- **Demo robot:** Sample Builds/Override Demo Robot.pbb, a legal Override robot on the 6-motor drive: drive into a lying
  pin or cup with the intake on (I) to pick it up, K to drop it; the arm (U/J) holds where you leave it.
- **Motors can hold:** a motor's card in Devices has Brake/Hold: Hold keeps an arm or lift where it stopped.
- **Play game:** the default start now finds a legal spot clear of the pins and cups by the wall (bigger robots started
  on top of them).
- **Parts list:** families open in place again, and their kinds open as smaller rows inside them.
- **Saw:** motors and electronics can't be cut (VEX's rules don't allow it).
- Fixed: the build tools showing during game setup after a run.

## Highlights
- **Full-detail VEX parts:** the whole VEX-CAD library, with a parts menu that opens each family in a panel beside the list.
- **Building:** quick fastening, parts that slide along screws and shafts, mirroring, saw, drill, bend tool, measure,
  paint, pneumatic tubing, V5 and 3-wire wiring, rubber bands, zip ties, polycarbonate sketcher, custom part import.
- **Motion:** motors, gears, chains, cylinders, hinges and slides that move, and a physics sandbox to drive the robot.
- **V5 Brain devices:** a Devices panel laid out like the Brain's screens: motor dashboards (gears, revolutions,
  velocity, power and torque), the 3-wire ports, and working sensors (distance, optical, light, line tracker, rotation,
  encoder, potentiometer, inertial, bumper and limit switches).
- **The game:** the 2026-27 V5RC Override field, Robot Skills and match practice with the manual's rules and scoring.
- **Online:** host or join matches by code, 2v2 with each player driving their own robot.

Protobot Rebuilt V2 continues Protobot (davegersh) and Protobot Rebuilt (BreadSoup). Not an official VEX product.
