{ping} 🍎 **Protobot is on Mac! (test version, and we need your help)**

Build 84 comes with a Mac version for Intel and Apple Silicon Macs: **ProtobotRebuiltV2-mac.zip** on the download page: <https://github.com/Cranezz/Protobot-Rebuilt/releases/latest>

**Heads up:** the developer doesn't have a Mac, so it has never been run on a real one. It might work perfectly, or parts of it might not work at all. If you have a Mac, please try it and tell us what happens!

**To open it:** unzip it, then **right-click the app and choose Open** the first time (it isn't notarized by Apple yet). If macOS says it's "damaged", open Terminal and run `xattr -cr ` followed by the app (drag the app into the Terminal window), then try again. Macs don't update by themselves: download new versions from the same page.

**What we'd love to hear:**
• Does it open? Which Mac (Intel or Apple Silicon, and the macOS version)?
• Building: parts list, placing parts, the tools, saving and opening files
• Simulate and Play game: does it run smoothly?
• Robot code, the VS Code button
• Multiplayer and voice chat
• Anything that looks wrong, is slow or crashes

Post what works and what doesn't in {#bugs} with the 🍎 **Mac** tag (screenshots help a lot), or use Help > Report a bug inside Protobot. Thank you, Mac testers! 🙏

Also new in build 84: a path planner for autonomous, a simpler match setup that lines your robot up on the tiles, chain tensioners, air in your pneumatic tanks, Toggles that flip, and what your robot costs. Full notes in {#releases}.
