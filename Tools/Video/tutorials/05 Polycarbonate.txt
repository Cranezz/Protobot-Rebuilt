# Tutorial 5: polycarbonate pieces, sketched on the robot (the developer's team's drivetrain corner pieces).
1 Polycarbonate makes custom guards, plates and covers. Let's make the corner pieces from this team's drivetrain: one over each end, around the wheel.
2 They stand on black nylon spacers, seven-eighths of an inch tall, so the plastic clears the cross bars and the wheels.
3 From the Plastic menu, open the sketcher and click Sketch on a part. Then click the top of a spacer: the sketch lies on it, and the view turns to look straight down.
4 Convert entities brings the spacers' holes into the sketch, so the piece gets a hole over each one.
5 A rectangle covers the end of the drivetrain, a slot leaves room for the wheel, and a fillet rounds the outside corner, just like in SolidWorks.
6 Click Make piece, then the area that makes the piece. Pick opal white plastic, and Create: it goes right onto the spacers.
7 The other three corners are the same piece. Mirror it with M, and drag the arrow to the middle of the robot: it snaps there.
8 Screws down through each hole hold it all together. Every piece counts toward the season's plastic limit, shown at the top. Next, gears and chains.
