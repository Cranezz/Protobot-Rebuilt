# Tutorial 3: the saw, the drill and the bend tool.
1 Real robots need parts cut to length, holes drilled, and plates and shafts bent. Protobot's saw, drill and bend tools do the same, on the parts in your build.
2 The saw is key four. Click a part, and a cut plane appears through its middle, with its distance from the end.
3 Drag the arrow to slide it, or type the distance you want in the bar at the top. Let's cut this channel five inches from the end.
4 Press Enter to cut. Both pieces are kept, each a part of its own, and cuts between holes give clean, standard parts.
5 The drill is key five. Pick a bit size at the top: scrolling over it steps through the sizes. Then click a hole to drill it out, round and to size.
6 The drill works on high strength shafts too. With a smaller bit, click the side of the shaft for a hole straight through it: it snaps to the middle.
7 Or click the end of the shaft to drill into its center, tapped for a screw, the way teams fasten a shaft end-on.
8 Bending works on plates, high strength shafts and standoffs. Press seven, and click the plate where the bend should be.
9 Type an angle, or drag the blue ring, then click Bend. The holes go round with the metal.
10 Every cut, hole and bend is saved with your build, and Control Z takes it back. Next, we'll measure parts and mirror them.
